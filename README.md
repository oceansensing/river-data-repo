# river-data-repo

Rivers and water gauges, as data for the oceansensing.org ocean map: the U.S.
Geological Survey's National Hydrography Dataset water — streams and rivers,
bays and inlets, canals and estuaries — where no NOAA nautical chart reaches,
as vector tiles cut to fit `enc-chart-repo`'s land; and the newest streamflow
and water level of the gauges of the USGS, the Water Survey of Canada, France,
England's Environment Agency (with the UK National Tide Gauge Network's
coastal gauges), Germany's federal waterways, Ireland's Office of Public
Works, Switzerland's Federal Office for the Environment, Sweden's SMHI and
Finland's Syke, and the typical flow for the day of thirteen Russian Arctic
river stations. The readings are provisional, and a typical flow is no
reading.

The gauges are updated hourly; the rivers when the charts' land is remade,
monthly.

## Sources and credits

The rivers: U.S. Geological Survey, the National Hydrography Dataset, a U.S. government work in the public domain.

The gauges: USGS's Water Data OGC API, a U.S. government work in the public domain. The Water Survey of Canada's stations, from Environment and Climate Change Canada's MSC GeoMet, OGL-Canada. France's: Hub'Eau's hydrometry API, eaufrance's, under Etalab's Licence Ouverte 2.0. England's Environment Agency real-time flood-monitoring API, the Open Government Licence v3.0 — *this uses Environment Agency flood and river level data from the real-time data API (Beta)*. Germany's PEGELONLINE, Datenlizenz Deutschland – Zero – 2.0. Ireland's waterlevel.ie, the Office of Public Works', re-use of public sector information, and only stations 00001 to 41000, as OPW asks. Switzerland's hydrodaten, the Federal Office for the Environment's, free to use with the source recommended. Sweden's SMHI open hydrological observations, CC BY 4.0. Finland's Syke Hydrology API, CC BY 4.0 — *Hydrologiarajapinta / Source: Finnish Environment Institute (Syke)*.

The Arctic rivers: the Arctic Great Rivers Observatory's discharge page, Roshydromet's daily discharge, free to use for any purpose with credit to ArcticGRO. Places from its discharge metadata, drainage areas from R-ArcticNET.
